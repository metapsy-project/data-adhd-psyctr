## **The `data-adhd-psyctr` dataset** 📊 

[<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/DOI-10.5281%2Fzenodo.8115993-blue">](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8115993) **UPDATE**

The `data-adhd-psyctr` dataset is a meta-analytic research domain [MARD](https://docs.metapsy.org/uploads/ebmental-2022-300509.pdf)) which is part of the  [Metapsy project](https://www.metapsy.org/). The dataset contains study information and effect size data of randomized trials on behavioral parent or teacher training for children and adolescents with ADHD under the age of 18 years. The date of the last search update is provided [here](https://github.com/metapsy-project/data-adhd-psyctr/blob/main/metadata/last_search.txt). A published meta-analysis using part of the data from this database can be accessed [here](https://acamh.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/camh.12561).

This dataset includes studies that compared parent or teacher training with a control condition, or studies that compared a multimodal intervention (i.e., a combination of parent, tea- cher, and/or child training) with a control condition. Reported outcomes are total ADHD symptoms, separate symptoms of inattention and hyperactivity/impulsivity, and behavioral problems (symptoms of oppositional defiant disorder [ODD], or conduct disorder [CD]). If no total ADHD symptom score were available, the separate inattention and hyperactivity-impulsivity symptoms scores are pooled and used this aggregate as a measure of total ADHD symptoms. If a study had multiple measurement points, the outcomes closest to the end date of the intervention were selected. If there were multiple informants, the parent-rated outcomes in case of parent training and the teacher-rated outcomes in case of teacher training were selected. For multimodal interventions, the rating of the informant with whom most training time was spent was selected. When parent-rated outcomes were reported by both parents, the mother-reported outcomes were selected to increase the comparability across studies. In case of multiple outcome measurements of the same construct, the measurement most commonly used across the included studies was selected.

The dataset follows the [Metapsy data standard](https://docs.metapsy.org/data-preparation/format/).
